# GTA Chandigarh — 3D Open-World Action & Driving Game

An original 3D open-world driving, traffic-discipline, police-pursuit, and mission sandbox game set in **Chandigarh — The City Beautiful**, inspired by Le Corbusier's iconic urban grid, roundabouts, brutalist architecture, and Indian street atmosphere.

## Key Features Implemented

### 1. Authentic 3D Chandigarh Open-World Map
- **Urban Grid & Arterials**: Explorable 1.6 km × 1.6 km city layout featuring **Madhya Marg**, **Dakshin Marg**, **Udyog Path**, **Jan Marg**, and **Himalaya Marg**, complete with 25 landscaped roundabouts (*Matka Chowk*, *Tribune Chowk*, *Sector 17 Chowk*, *Aroma Chowk*), synchronized traffic signals, sidewalks, streetlights, and avenue trees.
- **12 Recognizable Chandigarh Landmarks**:
  1. **Sector 17 Plaza**: Brutalist commercial arcades, musical fountain, and central courtyard.
  2. **Capitol Complex (Sector 1)**: Rotating **Open Hand Monument** & High Court / Secretariat slabs.
  3. **Nek Chand Rock Garden**: Curved terracotta arches and sculpture labyrinth.
  4. **Sukhna Lake Promenade**: Waterfront reservoir with animated floating Shikara boats.
  5. **Zakir Hussain Rose Garden (Sector 16)**: Multi-color rose beds and walking paths.
  6. **Panjab University (Sector 14)**: Iconic white geometric **Gandhi Bhawan** reflection pool & Student Centre (*StuC*).
  7. **ISBT Sector 17 Bus Terminus**: Central bus bays and commuter stands.
  8. **ISBT Sector 43 & District Courts**: Southern inter-state transport hub.
  9. **Elante Mall & Industrial Area Phase I**: Glass-and-steel shopping multiplex.
  10. **Chandigarh Junction Railway Station**: Rail terminal and clock tower.
  11. **Shaheed Bhagat Singh International Airport**: Glass terminal and runway corridor.
  12. **Sector 22 Shastri Market & Kothi Safehouse**: Player home base, customs garage, and street market.
- **Service Hubs & Collectibles**: 3 Petrol Pumps (*IndianOil*, *HP*, *Bharat Petroleum*), 2 Auto Customs & Respray Garages, 2 Safehouses, and **20 Golden Open Hand Trophies** hidden across the city.

### 2. Indian Vehicles & Arcade Driving Physics
- **9 Playable Indian Vehicle Classes**:
  - *Marut-800 DX* (Hatchback)
  - *Hindustan Royale / Dzire* (Executive Sedan)
  - *Sher-E-Punjab 4x4 Thar* (Heavy Gedi Route SUV with bullbar)
  - *Corbusier GT-X* (Twin-Turbo Luxury Sports Car)
  - *Chandigarh Tuk-Tuk Auto* (Green & Yellow 3-Wheeler Auto-Rickshaw)
  - *Royal Punjabi Cruiser 350* (Heavy thumping Motorcycle)
  - *CTU Low-Floor City Bus* (Heavy commuter transit bus)
  - *Chandigarh Police PCR Interceptor* (Emergency SUV with flashing beacons)
  - *PGIMER Rapid Ambulance 108* (Emergency response unit)
- **Full Vehicle Mechanics**: Acceleration, braking, reverse, handbrake drifting (`Space`) with tire skid audio, fuel consumption, collision damage, nitro boost (`NOS`), headlights (`F`), musical Indian horns (`H`), vehicle flip reset (`R`), and seamless on-foot / enter-exit vehicle switching (`E`).

### 3. Traffic Rules & 5-Level Police Wanted System
- **Traffic Enforcement**: CCTV chowk cameras detect **running red lights**, **excessive speeding (>110 km/h)**, **vehicle collisions**, and **reckless driving**.
- **5-Star Wanted Escalation**:
  - **Level 1 (Challan Warning)**: Traffic fine issued — pay an instant e-Challan (`P`) to clear heat.
  - **Level 2 (PCR Pursuit)**: Active Chandigarh Police PCR units pursue the player with sirens and beacons.
  - **Level 3 (Tactical Roadblock)**: Interceptor units calculate lead vectors to cut off escape routes.
  - **Level 4 (Heavy Pursuit)**: 6 coordinated units ram and box in the player vehicle.
  - **Level 5 (Citywide Lockdown)**: Maximum heat with 8 high-speed interceptors across all sectors.
- **Escape & Line-of-Sight**: Break police line-of-sight (135m radius), weave through sector backstreets, respray your vehicle at a Garage, or hide at a Safehouse until the search cooldown timer expires.

### 4. 32 Playable Missions (12 Core Missions + 20 Variations)
- **12 Core Missions**:
  1. *The Pickup* (Sector 17 to Sukhna Lake timed drop)
  2. *Traffic Discipline* (Sector 22 to Sector 17 obeying all red lights, 80 km/h speed limit, and zero crashes)
  3. *The Red Light* (Trigger CCTV camera pursuit and escape to Sector 22 Safehouse)
  4. *Airport Drop* (Panjab University to Chandigarh Airport express run)
  5. *The Delivery* (Fragile blueprints from Capitol Complex to Elante Mall)
  6. *Police Chase* (Survive a Level 3 ambush and reach Sector 8 Safehouse)
  7. *VIP Escort* (Zero-scratch dignitary chauffeur from Rose Garden to Secretariat)
  8. *Parking Challenge* (Precision <5 km/h stops inside 3 tight parking bays)
  9. *The Taxi Driver* (3 consecutive passenger pickups and drop-offs across town)
  10. *Sector Sprint* (5-checkpoint high-speed Gedi Route street race)
  11. *Emergency Response* (Ambulance rescue from Rock Garden to Sector 16 Hospital in monsoon rain)
  12. *The Getaway* (Grand finale heist escape through Railway Station to Airport Runway under Level 4 heat)
- **20 Additional Variations**: Monsoon Tuk-Tuk Dash, Midnight Gedi Sprint, Foggy Morning Challan Test, Student Centre Food Express, Shatabdi Express Connection, CTU Bus Corridor Tour, Perimeter Ring Rally, and more.

## Running Locally

### Option A: Zero-Setup Single-File Build
Open `gta_chandigarh.html` directly in any modern desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) with WebGL enabled.

### Option B: Modular Vite + React + Three.js Project
```bash
unzip gta_chandigarh_project.zip -d gta_chandigarh
cd gta_chandigarh
npm install
npm run dev
```

## Browser Engine Limitations & Unreal Engine 5 / Unity Migration Path
While this Three.js WebGL engine delivers 60 FPS procedural rendering, synthesized Web Audio, arcade vehicle physics, and 32 playable missions inside a browser sandbox, scaling to a commercial 100 GB AAA release has browser memory and shader constraints:
1. **Unreal Engine 5 Path**: Use **CitySample / MassAI** for 10,000+ simultaneous traffic agents and pedestrians, **Chaos Vehicle Physics** for multi-link suspension and deformable chassis meshes, **Lumen** for real-time global illumination off Sukhna Lake and Capitol Complex concrete, and **Nanite** for photogrammetry assets of Nek Chand's Rock Garden mosaics.
2. **Unity 6 HDRP Path**: Use **DOTS / Entity Component System (ECS)** for high-density Chandigarh traffic simulation, **WheelCollider / Edy's Vehicle Physics**, and **Cinemachine** for seamless cutscenes.
